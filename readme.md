### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm **Jairo Medrano**. I build the backend and everything around it: APIs, realtime, integrations with third-party services and the servers it all runs on. What I enjoy most is wiring together things that were never meant to talk to each other, from a GPS tracker over raw TCP to an electronic lock behind a REST API.

- 🧭 Tech lead on **[Whole Bible Community](https://wholebiblecommunity.com)** @ Higher Honduras: web, mobile and the video pipeline behind it.
- 🍳 Cooking up something new at **[jaw9.dev](https://jaw9.dev)**. Stay tuned.
- 🧠 AI-assisted workflows (Codex, Claude, Copilot) every day, reviewed like any other code.
- 🦀 Sharpening my English with US teams, and my Rust on the side.
- 🎧 Powered by chiptune, synthwave and classical music. And pancakes 🥞.
